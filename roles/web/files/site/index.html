runv.club

runv.club is a small public Unix server, run from Brazil. Members get an account on a shared Debian box and a corner of the small internet to go with it.

What you get:

  • a shell over SSH (or mosh), with editors, git, and Python, C, Go, Rust, Node, Ruby, Lua, Haskell and a few Lisps
  • a web page at runv.club/~you, a Gemini capsule and a Gopher hole
  • a blog: drop markdown in ~/blog and it's up on all three, and on the planet
  • your own address, you.tun.runv.club, for an app here or a tunnel from your laptop
  • an eepsite with its own address on I2P
  • git at git.runv.club, bookmarks at links.runv.club, an RSS reader at feeds.runv.club
  • #runv on tilde.chat through our IRC bouncer, also in the browser
  • pb for pastes, twtxt, finger, a forum in the terminal, and a dashboard: just type runv

It's a good place to learn your way around Linux and to put your writing online with no ads or feeds around it.

Every request to join is read by a person, and we let in 5 people a week. Ask to join, or from a terminal: ssh join@runv.club.

The old runv.club closed in October 2026. The letter is still up.
